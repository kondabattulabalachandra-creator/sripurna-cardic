A guide, not a diagnosis

Use this as a starting point for a conversation.

Heart anatomy and blood flow are explained here for general education. A website cannot assess your symptoms or decide which care is right for you. Please discuss personal questions with a qualified healthcare professional.

Possible emergency? Severe chest pain, sudden breathlessness, fainting or other possible heart attack symptoms need immediate emergency medical attention. Do not wait for a website reply or appointment.
